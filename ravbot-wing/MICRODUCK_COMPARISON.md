# Ravbot Wing and Microduck

This page exists because Microduck is an important reference point in the current small Physical AI robot ecosystem.

**Ravbot Wing is an independent project and is not affiliated with Pollen Robotics or Hugging Face.**

## What we are learning from the ecosystem

Microduck demonstrates useful ideas around reinforcement-learning locomotion, simulation-to-real workflows, compact Physical AI platforms, ONNX policy deployment, and community experimentation around learned robot skills.

Ravbot Wing does not aim to duplicate Microduck hardware.

## Different product direction

Ravbot Wing emphasizes:
- RK3588 onboard compute,
- local multimodal AI,
- voice + visual interaction,
- expressive wings,
- modular RavPort shoulders,
- future replaceable arms/hands,
- 3D-print-first community prototyping,
- a path from AI companion to manipulation platform.
